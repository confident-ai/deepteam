## Red team any AI app, wherever it runs.

Connect your AI app over HTTP and Confident AI simulates attacks against it directly, with no Python callback to maintain. Every assessment rolls up into a risk profile with CVSS scores and coverage, and every attack is kept as a test case you can triage.

- Connect any AI app, agent, or RAG pipeline through an HTTPS endpoint.
- Risk profiles with CVSS scores, pass rates, and coverage per vulnerability.
- Test cases for every attack, filterable by vulnerability, attack method, and status.

Fix what failed, then re-run the same assessment to confirm the risk is gone.
